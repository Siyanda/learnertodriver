## About Learner to Driver

### Is Learner to Driver free?
Yes. Signing up and taking quizzes are free. There are no paid plans, and we'll never ask for your card details.

### Is this the official learner's licence test?
No. Learner to Driver is an independent study tool. We aren't affiliated with the Department of Transport, the RTMC, or any driving licence testing centre (DLTC). Our questions are written to help you learn the material in the official K53 manual. They aren't copies of the real test.

### What if an answer here is different from the K53 manual?
The official K53 manual and the National Road Traffic Act always win. If you think one of our answers is wrong, please [let us know](/pages/contact) and we'll check it.

## Taking quizzes

### What do the quizzes cover?
The three sections of the learner's test: rules of the road; road signs, markings and signals; and vehicle controls.

### Are the quizzes timed?
Yes. Each quiz has a time limit so you can practise answering under pressure, like you will in the real test.

### Can I retake a quiz?
As many times as you like. Retaking the quizzes you struggled with is one of the best ways to prepare.

### Where can I see my results?
Sign in and open your dashboard to see the quizzes you've taken and how you scored.

## Using the app

### Do I need to install anything?
No. Learner to Driver runs in your web browser on any phone, tablet or computer. You can also add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:

- **Android (Chrome):** open the menu (⋮) and tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

### Does it work offline?
You need an internet connection to take quizzes and save your results.

### How much data does it use?
Very little. Most pages are text, so a quiz uses less data than a short video.

## Your account

### I forgot my password.
Use the **Forgot password?** link on the sign-in page. We'll email you a link to set a new one.

### I didn't get my confirmation email.
Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it isn't there, you can request a new confirmation email from the sign-in page.

Email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete your account and personal information. See our [Cancellation Policy](/pages/cancellation) for details.
