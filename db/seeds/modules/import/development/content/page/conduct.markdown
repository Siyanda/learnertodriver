## Be the kind of road user you'd want to share the road with

Everyone on Learner to Driver is working towards the same goal. Some people are taking the test for the first time, and some are trying again after a tough first attempt. This code of conduct applies wherever you can interact with other people here, including comments and profiles.

## Do
- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone starts somewhere. There are no stupid questions about road rules.
- **Help each other learn.** If you explain an answer, say *why* it's correct, and point to the K53 manual where you can.
- **Disagree respectfully.** Argue with the idea, not the person.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep comments relevant to the article or question.

## Don't
- Harass, insult, threaten or intimidate anyone.
- Post hate speech or attack anyone based on race, ethnicity, nationality, language, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other part of who they are.
- Post sexual, violent or graphic content.
- Share anyone's personal information without their permission.
- Spam, advertise, or post links to unrelated sites.
- Offer or ask for leaked test papers, "guaranteed pass" schemes, or bribes. Licence fraud is a crime, and it puts unprepared drivers on the road.
- Pretend to be someone else, including our team.

## What happens if someone breaks this code
Depending on how serious it is, we may remove the content, warn the person, or suspend or close their account. We decide case by case, and keeping the community safe comes first.

## Report a problem
If you see something that breaks this code, email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) with a link to it. We'll look into every report and keep the name of the person who reported it confidential.
