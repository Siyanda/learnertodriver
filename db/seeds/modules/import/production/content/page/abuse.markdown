## Use Learner to Driver to learn, not to cause harm

Learner to Driver is free for everyone. To keep it that way, you may not use it to:

- **Break the law**, including helping anyone commit licence fraud or cheat on an official test.
- **Copy or scrape our content.** That includes automatically downloading questions, answers or articles, or republishing them elsewhere, especially for profit.
- **Overload or attack the service.** That includes sending excessive automated requests, probing for vulnerabilities without permission, or trying to get around security measures.
- **Access what isn't yours.** That includes other people's accounts, admin areas, or data you weren't given access to.
- **Create accounts in bulk** or use fake identities to get around a suspension.
- **Spam or advertise**, including in comments, profile bios and profile links.
- **Upload harmful content** such as malware, or anything illegal, sexually explicit, violent or hateful.
- **Harass people.** See our [Code of Conduct](/pages/conduct).

## Found a security issue?
If you've found a vulnerability, please tell us privately at [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) instead of exploiting or publicising it. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will work with you to fix it.

## Enforcement
If we believe this policy has been broken, we may remove content, limit access, or suspend or close accounts, sometimes without notice if the risk is serious. Where the law requires it, we may report illegal activity to the authorities.

## Report abuse
Email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) with as much detail as you can.
