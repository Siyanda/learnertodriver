## No charges, no VAT

Learner to Driver is free to use. We don't sell anything, so we don't charge VAT and don't issue tax invoices.

## Fees for your test
Fees for your learner's licence test are paid to your driving licence testing centre (DLTC) or the relevant government booking service. They issue the receipts for those payments, not us.

## If that changes
If we ever introduce paid features, we'll explain on this page how VAT applies and how to get a tax invoice, before anyone is charged.
