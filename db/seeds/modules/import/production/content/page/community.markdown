## Learning is easier together

You're not the only one preparing for your learner's test. On Learner to Driver, you can read study articles and talk about them in the comments with other learners.

## Ways to take part
- **Ask questions** in the comments on a study article. If one rule has you confused, chances are someone else is wondering about it too.
- **Answer questions.** Explaining a rule to someone else is one of the best ways to remember it.
- **Share what worked.** Once you've passed, tell others how you prepared.
- **Report mistakes.** If a question or article looks wrong, [let us know](/pages/contact) so we can fix it for everyone.

## Getting help
- Check the [FAQ](/pages/faq) first. Your question may already be answered there.
- For account problems, [contact us](/pages/contact) directly instead of posting in the comments. Never post your email address, phone number or password publicly.

## Keep it friendly
Everyone here is learning. Please follow our [Code of Conduct](/pages/conduct): be patient, be kind, and keep comments on topic.
