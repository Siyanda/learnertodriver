## Leaving is easy

Learner to Driver is free, so there's no subscription to cancel and nothing to pay. If you've passed your test (congratulations!) or just don't want an account anymore, you can stop using it, or ask us to delete it completely.

## How to delete your account
Email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) **from the email address on your account** and ask us to delete it. To protect you, we won't delete an account based on a request from any other address.

## What happens next
- We'll confirm that we've received your request and delete your account within **30 days**.
- Your profile, quiz history and sign-in information will be permanently deleted.
- Comments you've posted will be deleted or anonymised.
- Copies in our backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

**Deletion can't be undone.** If you come back later, you'll start with a fresh account.

## Accounts we close
If we close an account because it broke our [Terms of Service](/pages/terms) or [Use Restrictions Policy](/pages/abuse), we'll let the account holder know by email where we can.

## Inactive accounts
We don't currently delete inactive accounts. If we ever start doing so, we'll update this page and email you before deleting yours.
