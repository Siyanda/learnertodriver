## Help more people get their licence

We'd like to work with organisations that care about new drivers and road safety in South Africa.

## Who we'd like to work with
- **Driving schools and instructors** who want their learners to arrive at lessons already knowing the rules of the road.
- **Schools, colleges and universities** that help young people get their first licence.
- **Non-profits and community organisations** working on road safety, youth employment or skills development.
- **Employers** helping their staff get the driver's licence a job requires.

## What we can offer
- A free, mobile-friendly study tool you can recommend to your learners, students or staff.
- Help planning content and quizzes around what your learners struggle with most.
- Credit on our site for partners who help us improve our content.

## What we won't do
To keep Learner to Driver trustworthy, we don't accept partnerships that involve selling user data, intrusive advertising, or anything that promises shortcuts around the official test.
