## Managing the account of someone who is incapacitated or has died

We're sorry if you're reading this because you've lost someone or because someone close to you is unwell. We'll keep this as simple as we can.

## What we can do
- **Delete the account** and all of the person's personal information.
- **Provide a copy of their information**, such as their profile and quiz history, where the law allows.

For the person's privacy, we can't give anyone else sign-in access to the account.

## How to make a request
Email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) with:

1. The person's full name and the email address on their account.
2. Your name and your relationship to them.
3. Proof of your authority to act for them, for example:
   - a death certificate, plus documents showing you're the executor or next of kin; or
   - a power of attorney or court order, if the person is incapacitated.
4. What you'd like us to do.

We'll handle your documents confidentially, use them only to process your request, and delete them once it's resolved.

## What happens next
We'll reply within a few working days. We may ask for more information if something isn't clear. Once we've verified your request, we'll do what you asked and confirm by email.
