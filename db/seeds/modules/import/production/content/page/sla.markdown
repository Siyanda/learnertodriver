## What you can expect from us

Learner to Driver is a free service run by a small team. We can't offer the contractual guarantees of a paid service, but here's what we aim for.

## Availability
- We aim to keep Learner to Driver available around the clock, every day.
- Planned maintenance is usually short. Where we can, we schedule it late at night, South African time.
- If something breaks, fixing it is our top priority.

Because the service is free, we don't offer credits or compensation for downtime. If it's down just before your test, the K53 manual is still the best fallback.

## Support
- We reply to emails sent to [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) within a few working days, usually sooner.
- Reports of incorrect questions and security issues jump the queue.

## Content accuracy
When someone reports a question that might be wrong, we review it, and if it is wrong, we fix it or take it down until it's corrected.

## Your data
