## How we protect your account and information

We only collect the information we need, and we take care of what we do collect. Here's how.

## Your account
- **Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.** We never store your password in a readable form, and nobody at Learner to Driver can see it.
- **Email confirmation.** New accounts and email address changes have to be confirmed from the inbox they belong to.
- **Secure password resets.** Reset links are single-use and expire after a short time.
- **Secure sessions.** The cookie that keeps you signed in is signed and can't be read by scripts on the page.

## In transit and at rest
- **HTTPS everywhere.** All traffic to Learner to Driver is encrypted, and plain HTTP requests are redirected to HTTPS.
- **Trusted providers.** Uploaded files are stored with Cloudflare, and account emails are sent through Postmark.
- **Backups.** We back up our database regularly so we can recover if something goes wrong.

## Inside our team
- Only a small number of people can use admin tools, and only to run the service and help users.
- We keep our software and its dependencies up to date and scan our code for common security issues.

## What you can do
- Use a strong password that you don't use on any other site. A password manager helps.
- Never share your password. We'll never ask you for it.
- Sign out on shared or public computers.

## Reporting a vulnerability
If you think you've found a security problem, email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) with the details. Please give us a reasonable amount of time to fix it before you share it publicly, and don't access other people's data while investigating. We'll reply, keep you updated, and thank you for helping keep learners safe.
