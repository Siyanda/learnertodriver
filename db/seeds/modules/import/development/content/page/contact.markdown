## We'd love to hear from you

Learner to Driver is run by a small team, and we read every message.

**Email:** [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za)

We usually reply within a few working days.

## What to include

Including these details helps us help you faster:

- **A question that looks wrong:** the quiz name, the question, and why you think the answer is wrong. If you can, point us to the relevant section of the K53 manual or the National Road Traffic Act.
- **Account problems:** the email address you signed up with. **Never send us your password.**
- **A bug:** what you were doing, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and the device and browser you were using.
- **Privacy requests:** to access, correct or delete your personal information, see our [Privacy Policy](/pages/privacy).

## Before you write

Many common questions are answered in our [FAQ](/pages/faq).

Please note that we're an independent study tool. We can't book tests, check test results, or help with licence applications. For those, contact your local driving licence testing centre (DLTC).
