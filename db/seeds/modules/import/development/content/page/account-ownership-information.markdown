## Who owns an account

A Learner to Driver account belongs to the person whose email address is on it. Whoever controls that email address controls the account, including the ability to reset its password.

Accounts are personal. Please don't share your account or sign up on someone else's behalf, unless you're a parent or guardian setting one up for a child under 18.

## What you own
- **Your content:** comments, profile details and anything else you add belong to you. See our [Terms of Service](/pages/terms).
- **Your quiz history:** this is your personal information, and you can ask for a copy at any time. See our [Privacy Policy](/pages/privacy).

The quizzes, questions and articles belong to Learner to Driver.

## Changing the email address on an account
You can update your email address from your profile page. We'll send a confirmation link to the new address before the change takes effect.

## Lost access to your email?
If you can no longer get into the email address on your account, email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za). We'll ask you some questions to confirm that the account is yours, such as when you signed up and which quizzes you've taken. We can't move an account to a new email address unless we're satisfied that you own it.

## Disputes
If two people claim the same account, we'll side with whoever controls the email address on it, unless there's clear evidence that it was taken without permission. In unclear cases, we may lock the account until the dispute is resolved.
