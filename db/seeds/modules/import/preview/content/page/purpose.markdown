## Why we exist

Every year, thousands of South Africans book a learner's licence test, pay the fee, take time off work or school, and fail. Often they don't know the rules. They studied in a way that didn't stick.

Learner to Driver exists to change that. We want more people to pass their learner's test the first time, and to know the rules of the road well enough to use them once they're behind the wheel.

## What we believe

- **Practice beats rereading.** You learn the rules by testing yourself and finding out why a wrong answer was wrong, not by reading the manual a fifth time.
- **Preparing should be free.** Your chances of getting a licence shouldn't depend on whether you can pay for a course. Learner to Driver is free to use.
- **Safer drivers make safer roads.** A learner's licence is the first step. People who understand the rules early become better drivers later, and South Africa's roads need them.
- **Plain language wins.** Road rules are confusing enough. We explain things simply and leave the jargon out.

## What we won't do

We won't sell your personal information, sell answers to the real test, or promise shortcuts. We'll help you learn the material properly. Passing the test is up to you.
