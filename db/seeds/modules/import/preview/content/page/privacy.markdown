_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

This policy explains what personal information Learner to Driver collects, why we collect it, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

The responsible party is **Learner to Driver**, which you can contact at [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za).

## What we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Account details:** your email address and password. We store your password as a one-way hash, so we never see the password itself.
- **Profile details (optional):** your name, username, title, bio, birthday, phone number, links and avatar.
- **Comments:** anything you post on study articles.
- **Messages:** anything you send us by email.

**Information we collect automatically**
- **Quiz activity:** the quizzes you take, your answers, your scores and when you took them.
- **Sign-in information:** when you sign in, how many times you've signed in, and the IP address and browser you signed in from.

## Why we use it
- To run your account and sign you in.
- To show your quiz results and progress.
- To send account emails such as email confirmation and password resets. We don't send marketing emails.
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
- To fix problems and improve our quizzes.

We **don't** sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Cookies
We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If you add the app to your home screen, your browser also stores a few files on your device so the app loads faster.

## Who we share it with
We use a few trusted service providers to run Learner to Driver:

- **Postmark** sends our account emails.
- **Cloudflare** stores uploaded files such as avatars.
- **Our hosting provider** runs the servers that store the database.

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa. When they do, we only use providers that are bound to protect it to a standard similar to POPIA. We'll also disclose information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
We keep your information while your account is open. When you close your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except for anything we must legally keep. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

## Your rights
Under POPIA you have the right to:

- ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
- ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date;
- ask us to delete your information;
- object to how we process your information;
- complain to the Information Regulator.

To exercise any of these rights, email [hello@learnertodriver.co.za](mailto:hello@learnertodriver.co.za) from the address linked to your account. You can also update most profile details yourself on your profile page.

## Children
Many learners are under 18. If you're under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian agrees to you using Learner to Driver. We only collect what we need to run the service.

## Security
See our [Security Overview](/pages/security) for how we protect your information.

## Changes
If we make significant changes to this policy, we'll let you know by email or with a notice on the site.

## Complaints
If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please talk to us first. You can also contact the **Information Regulator (South Africa)** at [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).
